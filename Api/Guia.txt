FLUJO MÍNIMO (3 PASOS)
1) JSON -> Odoo:
poetry run python import_products_to_odoo.py --json productos1.json

2) Levantar API:
poetry run uvicorn main:app --reload

3) Enviar Odoo -> PrestaShop (OBLIGATORIO):
$body = @{ limit = 20 } | ConvertTo-Json
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/sync/odoo-to-prestashop" -ContentType "application/json" -Body $body | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

Nota:
Los pasos 1 y 2 NO envían a PrestaShop por sí solos.

ENDPOINTS DEL FLUJO SOLICITADO:
1) Crear productos (GET):
GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/flow/create-products?limit=20

2) Crear producto por referencia (GET by reference):
GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/flow/create-product/by-reference/SKU-X

3) Actualizar producto en PrestaShop (GET by reference):
GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/flow/update-product/by-reference/SKU-X

4) Desactivar producto en PrestaShop (GET by reference):
GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/flow/deactivate-product/by-reference/SKU-X

4.1) Desactivar producto en ambas (Odoo + PrestaShop):
GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/flow/deactivate-product/both/by-reference/SKU-X

4.2) Reactivar producto en ambas (Odoo + PrestaShop):
GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/flow/activate-product/both/by-reference/SKU-X

5) Obtener órdenes de PrestaShop (GET):
GET http://127.0.0.1:8000/prestashop/orders?limit=20

correr script python (json -> odoo):
poetry run python import_products_to_odoo.py --json productos1.json

levantar la API:
poetry run uvicorn main:app --reload

===================================================
CONSULTAR ENDPOINTS EN TERMINAL (POWERSHELL)
===================================================

(en otra terminal) verificar productos de odoo por API:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/odoo/products?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 8

nuevos endpoints Odoo:
obtener ordenes:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/odoo/orders?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

obtener clientes:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/odoo/customers?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

obtener proveedores:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/odoo/vendors?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

obtener pagos:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/odoo/payments?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

obtener producto por SKU/CLAVE:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/odoo/products/by-sku/SKU-X" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

obtener orden por REFERENCE:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/odoo/orders/by-reference/S00001" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

(en otra terminal) verificar productos de prestashop por API:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/prestashop/products?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

obtener ordenes prestashop:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/prestashop/orders?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

obtener clientes prestashop:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/prestashop/customers?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

obtener orden prestashop por REFERENCE:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/prestashop/orders/by-reference/REFERENCIA" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

sincronizar odoo -> prestashop:
$body = @{ limit = 20 } | ConvertTo-Json
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Post -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/sync/odoo-to-prestashop" -ContentType "application/json" -Body $body | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

resultado esperado al sincronizar:
- synced > 0 si crea nuevos en prestashop
- updated > 0 si ya existían (upsert)
- errors debe venir []

ver trazabilidad de sincronizaciones:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/sync/logs?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10

ver mapeo persistente odoo<->prestashop:
Invoke-RestMethod -Method Get -Uri "http://127.0.0.1:8000/sync/mappings?limit=20" | ConvertTo-Json -Depth 10


variables opcionales en .env para robustez:
PRESTASHOP_RETRIES=2
PRESTASHOP_TIMEOUT=30
